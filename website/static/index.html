02 / DATA EXPLORATION

Explore your U–Th measurements

Visualize activity ratios and analytical uncertainties before running the Bayesian model.

Standardized activity ratios vs. depth

Each activity ratio is independently standardized using:

\[ Z_i = \frac{R_i - \overline{R}}{s_R} \]

Where \(R_i\) is the measured activity ratio, \(\overline{R}\) is the mean activity ratio, and \(s_R\) is its sample standard deviation.

We standardize to put all the activity ratios on a common scale. This helps to highlight potential trends, covariance, and anomalous measurements rather than looking at absolute values.
